Free VIN Decoder

What does this tool check?

Every vehicle sold in the U.S. since 1981 has a unique 17-character VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) that encodes its manufacturer, model, year, engine and more. This tool decodes that data directly from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's public database, then checks the same source for any open safety recalls affecting that exact make, model and year.

Is this really free?

Yes — no sign-up, no email, no hidden report fee.

How accurate is it?

Data comes from NHTSA's vPIC database, built from mandatory manufacturer filings — the most authoritative free source available.

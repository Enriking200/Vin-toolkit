How to Read a VIN Number

Every VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) is 17 characters long and follows a fixed structure defined by international and U.S. federal standards. Here's what each section means:

PositionSectionWhat it means
1–3WMIWorld Manufacturer Identifier — country and manufacturer.
4–8VDSVehicle Descriptor Section — body style, engine, model line.
9Check digitA calculated value used to detect invalid or mistyped VINs.
10Model yearA letter or number representing the model year.
11Plant codeWhich factory assembled the vehicle.
12–17Serial numberA unique sequential number for that specific vehicle.

You typically won't need to manually decode each position — that's what a decoder does automatically — but understanding the structure helps you spot an obviously invalid or altered VIN before running a check (for example, a genuine VIN never contains the letters I, O or Q, to avoid confusion with 1 and 0).
